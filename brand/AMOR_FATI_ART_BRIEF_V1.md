# Amor Fati: AI art handoff brief v1 (DRAFT)

Brand: philosophy-quote apparel where the whole garment tells the story of the phrase, like a painting. Brand name: TBD (placeholder [BRAND]).
Piece 1: *Amor fati* ("love of fate").
Status: 2026-10-19. Nothing spent, no accounts, nothing produced. This brief is for the user to paste into an image generator. The user sends the outputs back and we iterate.

## 1. The idea in one sentence

A single continuous image that moves from struggle to acceptance to love: you read it as you wear it. Not a front phrase plus a back graphic.

## 2. Story arc mapped to the garment (hoodie as the reference)

| Garment zone | What it shows | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Hem and lower body | Dark, rough storm sea, heavy waves, wreckage, a small boat tilting | Fate as hardship you did not choose |
| Mid torso (front and back) | The sea slowly calming. A figure stands at the boat's helm, hands on the wheel, facing the waves | Meeting fate instead of fleeing it |
| Upper chest and shoulders | Clouds break. Warm light rises; the water reflects gold | Acceptance turning into gratitude |
| Collar and hood | Sun or open sky, nearly pure light, a few birds | Loving fate: seeing it as the shape of your life |
| Sleeves | Rope, threads, or a red cord winding around the arm and tying to the boat and the sun | Fate as a thread you wear |
| Small hidden detail | Tiny embroidery or print at the inner collar: *Amor fati* in plain serif type | The phrase is the reveal, not the headline |

Key rule: the phrase is NOT written large on the garment. The image is the phrase.

## 3. Style direction

- Look: painterly, like a dramatic oil painting translated into clean, bold shapes that print well. Think old-master seascape drama mixed with modern poster clarity.
- Palette (limit to 5-6): deep navy, charcoal, storm gray, bone white, warm gold, a single muted red (the thread of fate).
- Rendering: strong light and shadow, visible brushstroke texture, no photorealism, no 3D render look, no glossy neon.
- Figure: seen from behind or in profile, no face, gender-neutral, so anyone can see themselves in it.
- Composition: a tall vertical image where the bottom is darkest and the top is brightest, with a clear flow from bottom to top. Keep important detail away from edges and seams.
- Do NOT copy any living artist, named painter's signature style, brand, character, logo, or watermark.

## 4. Prompt 1: master artwork (copy this)

> A tall vertical painterly artwork, a single continuous seascape that tells the story of "loving fate". At the bottom, a dark violent storm sea with towering waves and splintered wreckage, a small wooden boat tilting. Rising upward, the waves gradually calm; a faceless gender-neutral figure seen from behind stands at the helm, hands steady on the wheel, facing the storm without fear. Higher up, the clouds break open and warm golden light spills across the water. At the very top, a bright open sky with the sun and a few birds. A single thin muted-red cord winds through the whole image from the boat up to the sun, like a thread of fate. Style: dramatic oil-painting feel, bold readable shapes, visible brushstrokes, high contrast. Palette limited to deep navy, charcoal, storm gray, bone white, warm gold, and one muted red. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no signature, no frames, no borders, no photorealism, no 3D render, no neon. Composition vertical 2:3, strongest detail in the center, edges simple and uncluttered.

Negative prompt, if the tool supports it: text, letters, words, watermark, signature, logo, border, frame, photograph, 3D render, neon colors, extra limbs, distorted hands, face, crowd.

## 5. Prompt 2 and 3: sleeve and collar details

Sleeve (long narrow horizontal, repeat on both):
> A long narrow horizontal band of painterly art: a single muted-red cord winding in loose loops through calm gray-blue water and thin clouds, ending at a small sun at one end. Same style and palette as the master artwork. No text, no logos.

Collar and hood (round or square patch):
> A circular emblem of a bright golden sun over a calm sea with a thin muted-red cord crossing its center, painterly, same style and palette. No text, no logos.

## 6. Production notes (important)

- **Image generators misspell text and Latin.** Generate with no text. Add the phrase afterward in a design tool with a typeface the user picks.
- **Resolution and file limits** differ by print provider. Get the exact template and minimum pixel size from the provider before generating final art. Do not assume sizes. Upscale only after the user picks a winner.
- **All-over-print panels** (front, back, sleeves, hood) must be cut to the provider's template. Plan for the design to be continuous across seams; expect to adjust in a design editor.
- **Rights.** Check the generator's terms for commercial use before selling anything made with it. AI output may have limited copyright protection. A human-made or heavily human-edited final improves your position but is not a guarantee. The user should decide whether to hire an illustrator for the final art.
- **Trademark.** Check "amor fati" and the final brand name in the free USPTO search before printing.

## 7. How we iterate (user sends results back)

When the user sends the generator's output, I will judge it against this checklist and write the next prompt with specific changes:
1. Does the bottom-to-top story read in one glance (storm to light)?
2. Is the figure clear without a face and not cluttered?
3. Is the red thread visible and continuous?
4. Is the palette limited to 5-6 colors?
5. Are edges and seams free of important detail?
6. Is there any text, signature or logo that must be removed?
7. Does it feel like a painting, not clip art?

Then I change one or two things per round (for example composition, then palette, then figure) so we can see what helped.
