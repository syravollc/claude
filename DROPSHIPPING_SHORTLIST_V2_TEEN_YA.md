## 2. Decisions made (user delegated; recorded so they cannot be tuned later)

1. **No kids' products.** Children's items need US safety testing (CPSIA) that cheap imports often lack. Target buyer is a young adult, or a parent buying a teen gift. All listings state 14+ where small parts exist.
2. **Plan gates kept as written**, including US warehouse with 2-6 day delivery and no electrical or battery items. This rules out LED strips and star projectors, popular as they are. AliExpress direct shipping (often 7-20+ days) fails the gate and kill rule 5. Cheap suppliers that stock in US warehouses are acceptable; the user must confirm the warehouse location per product.
3. **Price gate kept ($30-80).** Single $10-20 trend items fail it, so each candidate is a **bundle or gift set priced at $30 or more**.
4. **No branded or lookalike characters or logos** (trademark gate 4). That excludes plush "collectible" lookalikes.

Recommendation: **B first**, then A, then C. Reasons, all judgment:
- B has the highest margin cushion and the strongest gift appeal in Q4.
- A is the lowest risk for returns and compliance.
- C is the most viral but most crowded; single items sell at $5-10 on Amazon, so the bundle must clearly look worth $32.

Risks:
- **B:** skin reactions and tarnish are common 1-3 star complaints on cheap jewelry. If Amazon reviews show a recurring plating/allergy complaint, gate 5 rejects it unless the supplier offers stainless steel or hypoallergenic material. Avoid any "hypoallergenic" claim you cannot document.
- **A:** easy to copy, so price pressure from Amazon and Etsy. Bundle uniqueness matters.
- **C:** price anchor against $8 listings; saturated ads.
- **All:** at the arithmetic floor they need roughly 2.7-3.3% conversion on cold traffic at $0.60 CPC. Q4 clicks may cost more, so some candidates may fail on numbers before any ad runs.
