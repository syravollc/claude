# Tee design concepts (DRAFT, text briefs only)

Status: 2026-10-14. Nothing spent, no accounts, no artwork produced. These are written briefs, not finished designs. Gate exception for print-on-demand delivery time (see `DROPSHIPPING_SHORTLIST_V2_TEEN_YA.md` section 8) is **not yet approved** by the user.

## Rules every concept follows

- Original wording and original art only. No brand names, characters, logos, song lyrics, show or game references, or celebrity names.
- Trend cues are style only: Y2K, coquette, study-aesthetic, soft pastel. No copying of any existing shirt.
- Slogans are short common phrases or invented ones. Common phrases can still be trademarked or already on sold shirts. **Check every slogan** in the USPTO trademark search (free, tmsearch.uspto.gov) and on Amazon/Etsy/Redbubble before use.
- Artwork source must be one the user can legally sell: drawn by the user or a hired freelancer under a written commercial-use agreement, or a stock/AI tool whose license allows commercial print. AI-generated art may have weak copyright protection and platform restrictions. Check the terms of whatever tool is used. Do not trace or "style-match" a named artist.
- No health, identity, political or age claims. Target buyer 16+ for the teen-gift angle; ads speak to young adults or parents, not minors.

## Concept 1: "Late Night Library Club"

- Look: cream or dusty-blue tee. Front-center badge: stacked books, a pencil with a small bow, a crescent moon, tiny stars. Arched text above: LATE NIGHT LIBRARY CLUB. Small line below: EST. FINALS WEEK.
- Palette: 3 colors (butter yellow, soft pink, navy line). Flat vector, no gradients.
- Appeals to: students, "study with me" audience, journaling fans.
- Ad angle: "For everyone still studying at 1 a.m." gift for a student.
- Risk: moderate. The study aesthetic is crowded, so the badge illustration must look distinct.

## Concept 2: "Sunday Reset"

- Look: white or baby-pink tee. Bubble-style chrome-look lettering SUNDAY RESET, with 3 hand-drawn butterflies and a few sparkle shapes. Small tagline on back neck: tidy desk, clean slate.
- Palette: silver-gray, pink, pale blue on light tee. Keep it flat; fake chrome gradients print poorly, so use vector shading in 2-3 steps.
- Appeals to: Y2K style fans and the "reset routine" content crowd.
- Ad angle: lifestyle video of a Sunday routine wearing the tee.
- Risk: Y2K chrome lettering is very common; a "reset routine" slogan may already be in use. Run the name check.

## Concept 3: "Quiet Hours"

- Look: heather gray or black tee. Small chest print (left) of an open notebook with a pencil and a crescent moon, lines as thin outline. Large back print: QUIET HOURS in clean serif type, tiny line below: open at midnight.
- Palette: single-color print (white on dark, or dark on light). Single-color prints are the cheapest to produce and the least likely to look wrong.
- Appeals to: introverts, night owls, minimal-style buyers (broadest audience of the three).
- Ad angle: gift for the friend who stays up writing, reading, or studying.
- Risk: low art risk, higher "plain shirt" risk; it needs a strong mockup and video to stand out.
