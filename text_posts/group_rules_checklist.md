# Before you post in any group or community

Do this every time. Communities remove accounts that only promote.

1. **Read the rules** (pinned post, about section). Many ban links, self-promotion, or business posts.
2. **Ask the admin first** if you'd like to share a free resource. Use Message C in `launch/network_outreach.md`. Wait for a yes.
3. **Be a member before you're a marketer.** Comment helpfully for a few weeks before sharing anything of your own.
4. **Answer the actual question.** Use your own words and your own experience, not a copy-paste.
5. **No link in the first reply** unless allowed. Offer to share by message instead.
6. **Be honest and humble.** Say "my experience" and "every child is different." No guarantees.
7. **Don't speak for other families.** No private details about parents, brothers or anyone else.
8. **Disclose.** If you mention your resource, say it's yours.
9. **Don't post the same text in many places** in a short time. It looks like spam.
10. **Log what you learn** in the Problems Log tab of `outreach_tracker.xlsx` (the common questions are your product ideas).
11. **If a post is removed or someone objects, stop and apologize.** Don't argue.
12. **Platform rules change.** Check them again each month.
