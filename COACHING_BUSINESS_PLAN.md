# Coaching + course business plan: accelerated learning for homeschool families

Status: DRAFT. Nothing is built, spent or launched. All prices and numbers below are hypotheses to test, not researched market rates.

## 1. What you are selling

You, as someone who finished high school at 13 and college at 17, helping families decide if, when and how to accelerate, and how to do it without burning out. The product is clarity and a plan, not guaranteed outcomes.

Be honest on the page about what you are: a person with lived experience, not (unless true) a licensed teacher, counselor or psychologist. Do not give medical, mental-health or legal advice. Refer out when needed. State this in your offers and on calls.

## 2. Offer ladder (build in this order)

| Step | Offer | Hypothesis price | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Free: 10-Question Readiness Check (2 pages) | Free | Collect emails, show expertise |
| 1 | Guide + planner (see `GUIDE_OUTLINE.md`) | $19-29 | First purchase, builds trust |
| 2 | 1-on-1 "Acceleration Roadmap" session (45-60 min, with written plan) | $75-150 | Fastest cash; learn what families need |
| 3 | Small-group course / cohort (4-6 weeks, live Q&A + templates) | $149-399 | Scalable once Step 2 teaches you the content |
| 4 | Ongoing coaching / membership | $49-99 per month | Recurring revenue, only after Step 3 works |

Test each price with real buyers; change only between rounds, never mid-round.

## 3. Audience: where families come from

Everything starts with the free checklist and an email list you own.

1. **Content (faceless, animated, your own voiceover optional):** 4 posts a week from the pipeline (`content_pipeline/`). Topics come from real parent questions.
2. **Communities:** answer questions in homeschool groups and forums, following each group's rules. Help first; links rarely.
3. **Search/Pinterest:** printable planner previews and "readiness checklist" pins pointing to the landing page.
4. **People you already know:** tell everyone in your network you are offering sessions. Warm contacts are the fastest first customers.
5. **Partnerships:** guest spots with homeschool podcasts, newsletters or co-ops (ask; many say no).

Faceless caveat: 1-on-1 sessions and live Q&A need your voice at minimum. Options: audio-only calls, or video calls with a plain background. Families will want to hear who they are paying. Decide what you are comfortable with now.

## 5. 90-day timeline

**Days 1-14: foundation**
- Fill in `content_pipeline/story_facts.md`.
- Write the free checklist and publish the landing page and email signup.
- Post the first 8 pieces of content; tell your network about the sessions.
- Write a one-page session offer (who it is for, what happens, what you do not do, price, refund policy).

**Days 15-45: first revenue**
- Run 5-10 discovery chats or paid sessions. Take notes: exact questions, worries, words parents use.
- Finish and launch the guide to the list.
- Keep posting 4 times a week. Review analytics weekly.

**Days 46-90: scale what works**
- Turn the most common session topics into the course outline.
- Pre-sell a first small cohort (a handful of seats) before building the full course.
- Add testimonials only from real clients with their written permission.

## 6. Success gates and kill rules (set now)

| By day | Target | If missed |
|---|---|---|
| 30 | 100+ email subscribers and 5 real conversations with parents | Change the hook/topic and offer; do not add tools |
| 60 | 3 paid sessions or 15 guide sales | Review price, message and audience; one adjustment round |
| 90 | 10 paid sessions or 30 guide sales, and 200+ on the list | Reassess honestly; consider a different offer or stop |

Hard stops: cash spent reaches $300, an accuracy or safety complaint you cannot resolve, a platform strike, or any pressure to promise results.

## 7. Illustrative income paths (hypothetical, not forecasts)

- 10 sessions a month at $100 = $1,000 a month.
- 30 guide sales a month at $19 = about $450 net.
- One cohort of 20 students at $199 = $3,980 per cohort.
- $10,000 a month would need a combination of these sustained, for example 25 sessions, 2 cohorts and a recurring base, which is a lot of hours and an established audience. Treat it as a long-term goal, not a 90-day expectation.

## 8. Risks and how to handle them

- **No audience early on:** start with warm contacts and communities, not only content.
- **Trust and credentials:** be specific and documented about your story; show diplomas and transcripts with personal details blurred; do not exaggerate.
- **Children's data and safety:** do not collect minors' sensitive data; keep records minimal; have parents book sessions; set clear boundaries; check local rules for working with minors.
- **Regulation:** check education-service, tax and business-registration rules where you live.
- **Burnout:** cap sessions per week.
- **Single point of failure (you):** the guide, list and cohort course are what make income less dependent on your hours.
