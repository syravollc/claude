How to use this planner
  1. Fill in the Year-at-a-Glance together.
  2. Copy the Weekly Planner each week (print several).
  3. Do the Monthly Check-In on the same day each month.
  4. Keep the Records Tracker updated every term.
  5. Use the College Questions page before talking to any college.
  6. Use the Reset Plan when things feel stuck.

Print on regular paper. Pencil works best, because plans change, and that is the point.

Reset Plan

Use when everything feels stuck. A rough stretch is a signal to adjust, not a failure.

What's been hardest
What we're pausing
What we're keeping
The one thing we'll change
Who we'll talk to (trusted adult, mentor, professional)
Steps
  1. Stop adding new commitments for two weeks.
  2. Have one honest conversation.
  3. Protect sleep, play and friends.
  4. Pick one thing to change.
  5. Recheck in two weeks.
  6. Decide together: continue, adjust or pause.
Review date

If your child seems consistently sad, anxious, withdrawn or unsafe, or you are worried about their health, please contact a doctor, counselor or other qualified professional.
