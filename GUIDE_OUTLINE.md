# Guide outline (DRAFT): Accelerated Learning for Homeschool Families

Working title options (test with a poll before committing):
1. *Early, Not Rushed: A Parent's Guide to Accelerated Homeschooling*
2. *From High School at 13: What I'd Tell Your Family*
3. *The Accelerated Homeschool Roadmap*

Format: PDF, about 35-50 pages, plus a printable planner (8-12 pages). Price to test: $19 (see workbook).
Voice: first-person, honest about trade-offs. Not a promise of results.

## How to use this outline
- `[YOUR STORY]` marks places only you can fill in with true, documentable facts (dates, courses, what was hard). Do not let AI invent any personal detail.
- `[VERIFY]` marks facts that must be checked against a primary source (state law, college policy, test rules) before publishing.
- Every chapter ends with one concrete tool (checklist, worksheet or decision guide) so the guide is useful, not just a story.

## Front matter
- **Who this is for / who it is not for.** Families considering acceleration, not families who want a pressure plan.
- **What this guide is and is not.** Based on one person's experience plus research; not legal, medical or educational advice; every child is different.
- **About the author.** [YOUR STORY: graduated high school at 13, college at 17. Add your documentation: diplomas, transcripts, dates. Keep it factual.]

- [YOUR STORY: what the timeline actually looked like, year by year.]

## Build workflow (AI drafts, you review)
1. You write the `[YOUR STORY]` sections yourself, even roughly. AI only polishes.
2. AI drafts the generic sections and tools from this outline.
3. You verify every `[VERIFY]` item against a primary source and delete anything unverified.

## Claims to avoid
- "Your child can graduate by 13" or any guaranteed outcome.
- Advice about disabilities, mental health or medical issues beyond "consult a professional."
- State-by-state legal summaries unless each state is verified.
- Testimonials you cannot back with real names or permission.

## Decisions made (from the author)
- Open book: willing to share the full story (documented in `content_pipeline/story_facts.md`).
- Audience: both parents and students. Add a short "for students" box at the end of each chapter.
- Tone: warm, personal, relatable.
- Presentation: faceless brand with animated videos. Credibility comes from specific story detail, documents shown with personal info blurred, and optionally your own voiceover.

## Questions for you before drafting
- Which parts of your story are you comfortable sharing, and what documents can you show?
- Is the guide for the parent, the student, or both?
- Preferred tone: warm and personal, or practical and checklist-driven?
