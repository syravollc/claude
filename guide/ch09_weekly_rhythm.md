## Principles
1. **Start with the learner.** Morning person or night owl? Short bursts or long blocks? Ask.
2. **Build around the child's learning style.** If lectures don't work, don't make them the backbone.
3. **Protect rest.** Breaks, sleep, play.
4. **Plan the social side.** Put it on the calendar.
5. **Leave slack.** A packed schedule breaks the first bad day.
6. **Review, then adjust.** Every month, change one thing.

## Sample shapes (adapt freely)
**A. Focused mornings**
- Morning: two focused blocks (hardest subjects).
- Midday: break, lunch, movement.
- Afternoon: lighter work, projects, reading.
- Late afternoon/evening: activities, friends, rest.

**B. Project days**
- Two or three days of structured subjects.
- One or two project days (deep work on one interest).
- One day for community activities and catch-up.

**C. Online instructor + own pace**
- Fixed times for instructor sessions or check-ins.
- Own-pace work in flexible blocks.
- Weekly review with a parent.

Pick the one that fits and change it as you learn.
