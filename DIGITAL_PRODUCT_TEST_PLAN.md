# Digital product test plan: homeschool / accelerated learning

Status: DRAFT. Nothing here has been run or spent. Replaces the physical-dropshipping plan for this niche (`DROPSHIPPING_TEST_PLAN.md` stays for reference). Companion workbook: `digital_product_break_even.xlsx`.

## 1. Purpose and honesty clause

Answer one question cheaply: will homeschool parents pay for a guide or bundle built on your accelerated-learning experience, through a content-first launch?

- A pass earns a second round, not a business. About 30 buyers cannot prove a stable income.
- The base-case funnel in the workbook falls short of a pass. Expect failure or a weak result; it is a planned, cheap cost.
- This niche runs on trust. Cold paid ads on a low-priced digital product usually cost more per sale than the sale is worth, so ads are a late, optional step.
- Claims must be ones you can back up. No promised outcomes for children.

## 2. Hard limits (set now, do not change mid-test)

| Item | Limit |
|---|---|
| Total cash cap | **$300** ($100 fixed costs + up to $200 ads) |
| Duration | 8 weeks |
| Time cap | 10 hours per week |
| Products | 1 guide plus an optional printable planner |
| Who approves spend | The human. AI agents never hold a card or ad credentials |

## 3. Phase 0: demand gates (week 1, near $0)

All must pass before building the product.

1. Google Trends (12 months) shows steady or rising interest for 3+ terms, e.g. "accelerated homeschool", "early graduation homeschool", "dual enrollment homeschool".
2. Etsy and Teachers Pay Teachers show competing products at $15-25 with real reviews, and you can name what yours does differently.
3. You can state your story with facts you can document (dates, diplomas, transcripts). Keep a file of evidence.
4. Parent-question list: at least 10 real questions from forums or comments (e.g. when to accelerate, burnout, gaps, social readiness, dual enrollment) that your guide answers.
5. Legal and platform basics checked: store terms, refund policy, tax treatment of digital sales where you live, and state homeschool laws if the guide mentions them. Meta Advertising Standards read directly before any ad.
6. Net contribution per sale M is positive in the workbook.

## 4. Break-even economics

```
price                 P  = $19  (example)
platform fee          10% of P   (assumption: varies by platform)
payment fee           2.9% of P + $0.30
refund reserve        5% of P    (assumption)
net per sale          M  = P - fees - reserve = $15.30
```

- Sales to cover $100 fixed costs: about 7.
- Cold-ad viability: ads need cost per sale at or below 0.7 x M, about $10.70. At $0.80 per click that needs about 7.5% click-to-purchase. Typical cold traffic converts far lower, so ads are expected to fail unless warm audiences (list, visitors) convert well.

## 5. Phases

**Phase 1: build and warm up (weeks 1-4)**
- AI drafts the guide and a free lead magnet; you review every page for accuracy and honesty.
- Publish 3-5 short pieces of content per week. Collect emails through the lead magnet.
- Landing page and checkout ready before week 5.

**Phase 2: launch (weeks 5-8)**
- Launch to the list and your content audience at the pre-set price. No price changes mid-test.
- Track weekly: posts, views, signups, list size, customers, refunds.

**Phase 3: optional paid test (only if 10+ organic sales)**
- Up to $200, retargeting or warm audiences first. Cold ads only if the workbook shows they can clear 0.7 x M.

## 6. Kill rules (any one stops the test)

1. Cash spent reaches $300.
2. Week 3 or later, 12+ posts published, fewer than 25 email signups.
3. Three weeks after launch, 100+ on the list, fewer than 5 sales.
4. Refund rate above 10% (with 10+ sales), or any accuracy complaint you cannot resolve.
5. Any ad spend before 10 organic sales.
6. Platform strike or ad-account warning.
7. Week 8 reached.

On a kill: stop spending that day, write what you learned, do not extend.

## 7. Week-8 decision

| Outcome | Condition | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Pass** | 30+ customers, list 200+, refund rate under 8%, net profit positive | Plan a second round at a pre-written cap |
| **Inconclusive** | 10-29 customers | One more capped round, same rules |
| **Fail** | under 10 customers | Stop and archive |

Timing note: peak homeschool buying is reportedly August-September, so a fall launch is off-season. A weak first result may reflect timing, not the idea. Keep this in mind when reading an Inconclusive result.

## 8. What AI automates and what stays human

| Task | AI | Human |
|---|---|---|
| Research, outlines, first drafts, printable layouts | Yes | Reviews every page |
| Video scripts, captions, email copy | Drafts | Approves |
| Weekly numbers report | Yes | Reads |
| Your story and claims, pricing, refunds, anything about children's outcomes | No | Yes |

## 9. Limits of this plan

- Market-size and price figures come from secondary blogs, not primary data.
- No cost-per-click or conversion benchmark for education products was found; the workbook uses assumptions.
- Taxes and your time are not modelled.
