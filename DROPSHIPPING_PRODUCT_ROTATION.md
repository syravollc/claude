# Product rotation system and candidate pool (DRAFT)

Status: 2026-10-14. Research and planning only. Nothing spent. Written for the user's request to find the best products and to switch products as trends change instead of sticking with one.

## 1. What the research could and could not find

- **Could not find:** a verified "best product." No live TikTok Shop ranking (Kalodata, FastMoss), Amazon Movers and Shakers snapshot, Google Trends, or supplier price list is reachable from here. Search returned vendor and seller-tool blogs, whose claims are anecdotal.
- **Found (low-quality evidence):** repeated Q4 categories in seller blogs: home and cozy items, kitchen gadgets, pet supplies, jewelry and personalized gifts, beauty sets, power banks, trackers, candles, seasonal decor. One seller reports a $39.99 heated blanket with $11.20 supplier cost, but that is one seller's 2025 anecdote.
- **Success odds:** the most repeated figure is 10-20% of dropshippers reaching sustained profit, with 5-15% net margin after ads. These come from vendor blogs and measure abandonment as much as failure. No real customer acquisition cost data was found.
- **Conclusion:** there is no reliable way for me to name "the absolute best" product. What I can build is a repeatable way to screen and rotate products cheaply.

## 2. Important conflicts with the current plan

1. Many of the Q4 blog favorites (heated blankets, milk frothers, LED lights, handheld vacuums, candle warmers, power banks, trackers) are **electrical or battery** items. Plan gate 4 excludes them. Reasons are real: fire/shock liability, certification (UL/FCC), and higher returns. Recommendation: keep the exclusion.
2. "Switch based on trends" must still obey the kill rules. Switching is allowed **between tests**, not mid-test, because rule 7 forbids tuning after seeing results. The $400 cap covers all products combined unless the user writes a new cap in the plan log first.
3. Q4 is half over. Hyped Q4 products have saturated ad auctions and tight delivery cutoffs.

## 3. Rotation rules (to be added to the plan only with the user's approval)

- One active product at a time. Each product gets a mini-test: 14 days or $100 ad spend, whichever comes first.
- Switch when a kill rule fires, or when the trend score (section 4) falls below 12 for two weekly checks in a row.
- Never switch because of one bad or good day.
- Every new product passes the full pre-spend gates again, with a log entry before spending.
- Keep a bench of 3 pre-screened products so a switch costs days, not weeks.

## 4. Weekly product scorecard (user fills; AI summarizes)

Score each 0-3, total 18:

| Criterion | 0 | 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Margin at $30-80 price | <50% | >=65% |
| US-warehouse delivery | none | confirmed 2-5 days |
| Search trend (Google Trends 12 mo.) | spike or falling | rising or steady |
| Ad saturation (Meta Ad Library) | 50+ near-identical ads | demand with few advertisers |
| Review defects (Amazon 1-3 star) | recurring defect | none recurring |
| Return/compliance risk | fragile, electrical, claims | durable, unregulated |

Proceed to a test only at 14+ with no zero in margin, delivery or compliance.
