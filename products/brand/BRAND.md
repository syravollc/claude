## 1. The name

**Brand: Early, Not Rushed**  
**Etsy shop name (one word, no spaces): EarlyNotRushed** (14 characters)

Why it fits:
- It says the thing parents fear and the thing you lived: starting early without cutting corners. Your story is the proof, so the name does the work of the pitch.
- It is the title of the guide, so shop, product and story all match.
- It is not tied to "homeschool" or "college", so it can widen later (teens, study skills, career planning) without a rename.
- It reads as calm, not hype. Good for parents who are wary of "genius kid" marketing.

## 5. Voice and taglines

Voice: warm, honest, plain. First person. Says what worked and what did not. Never promises outcomes, never pressures.

Tagline options (pick one and keep it):
1. Learning that fits. *(used in the logo lockup)*
2. Early is fine. Rushed is the problem.
3. A plan for families who want to start early, calmly.
4. From a student who did it, for families deciding.

Avoid: "genius", "fast-track to success", "guaranteed", "secret".

Printable planners, readiness checklists and a first-year starter pack for homeschool and early-college families.
