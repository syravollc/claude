## E. How to approach each
1. **Spend 10 minutes** reading the page, rules and a few recent posts or episodes.
2. **Groups:** Message C from `launch/network_outreach.md` (ask first).
3. **Podcasts and bloggers:** short pitch (below).
4. **Follow up once** after about 10 days, then let it go.
5. **Never mass-send** identical messages. Personalize one specific line referencing something they published.

### Pitch template (podcast or blog)
Subject: Guest idea: finishing high school at 13 and college at 17, and why early isn't the goal

Hi [name],
I enjoyed [specific episode/post], especially [one real detail]. I'm Fabian Capre. I was homeschooled from 3rd grade, finished high school at 13 through FLVS Flex, earned my associate degree at 15 and my bachelor's at 17, and I'm now working on a master's. I'd love to share what worked, what was hard, and why early graduation isn't the only outcome, in a way that's useful to your audience.
Possible topics: (1) the first year of online school and finding community, (2) how to know if a child is ready vs. pushed, (3) starting college early: what to ask. I can also offer a free checklist for your listeners/readers.
I'm happy to send my bio, and I can show my diploma and transcripts. No pressure at all.
Thanks, Fabian
