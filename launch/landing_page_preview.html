What I've learned

It can bend to the child.
Face to face with an instructor, online with one, or no lectures at all. Build around how your child learns.
Ask for feedback, then use it.
I had a say in my own education. That changed everything.
Social life needs a plan.
I was in local high school clubs, organizations and church groups, and had as much social life as anyone, maybe more.
Starting hard is normal.
My first year of online school was the hardest part. It got better.
Don't recreate school at home.
I've seen families run homeschool just like in-person school, in the living room. It doesn't have to be that way.
