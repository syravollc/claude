# The Acceleration Roadmap session (draft offer)

Price hypothesis: $75-150. Test one price per round (suggest starting at the low end for the first 5 sessions, then adjust). Fill in once chosen.

## Who it's for
Parents (and students, if the family wants them on the call) who are considering homeschooling, moving to a flexible/online path, or accelerating, and want help thinking it through.

## What happens
1. **Before the call (10 min):** you fill in a short form: your child's age and current setup, what's working, what isn't, and what you'd like to be different.
2. **The call (45-60 min):** we go through your answers, talk about learning styles and options, discuss social life, pace and next steps. I share what worked for me and what I'd do differently.
3. **After the call:** you get a one-page written roadmap with 3-5 concrete next steps and questions to ask schools or programs.

## What I do not do
- I'm not a licensed teacher, counselor, psychologist or lawyer. I don't diagnose, give medical or mental-health advice, or tell you what your state requires.
- I don't promise any outcome, including early graduation.
- I can point you to where to look for official rules (your state education department, your district, the program you're considering).

## Minors
Sessions are booked and paid for by a parent or guardian. A parent or guardian should be on the call. I keep notes minimal and don't need names, records or identifying details about your child.

## Policies (suggestions to decide on)
- Cancellation: free to reschedule with 24 hours' notice.
- Refund: if the session isn't useful, tell me within 7 days and I'll [refund / offer a follow-up]. (Decide before launch and keep it.)
- Recording: calls are not recorded unless you agree in writing.
- Privacy: [link]. Notes deleted after [X] days.

## Intake form (questions)
1. Learner's age or grade (no name needed).
2. Current setup (public school, private, homeschool, virtual, other).
3. What's working now?
4. What isn't?
5. How does the learner like to learn (live instructor, online instructor, reading, hands-on, other)?
6. What are you hoping to change in the next 6 months?
7. What worries you most?
8. Anything you'd like me to know or avoid?

## Booking and payment setup (verify fees before choosing)
- Scheduling: Calendly or Cal.com with the intake form attached.
- Payment: Stripe or PayPal, charged at booking.
- Calls: Zoom or Google Meet. Audio-only is fine if you prefer.
- Confirmation email: includes what to expect, the "what I do not do" list, and the refund policy.

## Listing blurb (one paragraph)
60 minutes with someone who left in-person school in third grade and graduated high school at 13 and college at 17. We'll talk through your family's learning style, pace, social life and options, and you'll leave with a one-page roadmap. Not a guarantee of any outcome, and not professional advice.
