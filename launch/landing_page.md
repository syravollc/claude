## What I've learned
- **Homeschooling can bend to the child.** Face-to-face with an instructor, online with one, or no lectures at all. The point is to build around how your child learns.
- **Ask for feedback and use it.** I had a say in my own education. That changed everything.
- **Social life is real, but it needs a plan.** I was in local high school clubs and organizations and church groups, and I had as much social life as other kids, maybe more.
- **Starting hard is normal.** My first year of online school was the hardest part, and I hated it. It got better.
- **Don't recreate school at home.** I've seen families run homeschool exactly like in-person school, just in the living room. It doesn't have to be that way.
