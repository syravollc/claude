**Your choices:** You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in my emails, and you can ask me to see or delete your information by emailing [EMAIL].

**Security:** I use reputable services and take reasonable care, but no system is perfectly secure.

**Changes:** I may update this policy and will change the date above.

**Not professional advice:** My content and chats are general information from personal experience, not educational, psychological, medical or legal advice.
