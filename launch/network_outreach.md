# Network outreach (first customers): drafts

Goal: 5 real free conversations with parents in 30 days (free-only mode). Paid sessions come later. Send one-to-one messages to people you actually know or who have asked. No mass blasts, no pressure. Change every message to sound like you.

## Rules
- Personal messages only. If someone says no or doesn't reply, don't chase.
- Don't promise outcomes. Say what you offer and what you don't.
- Keep proof ready (diploma, transcripts, degree) if asked.
- Don't share details about your parents or brothers beyond what they've okayed.

## Message A: someone who knows you (family friend, church, club contact)
Hi [Name], hope you're doing well! I'm starting something new: I'm helping families think through homeschooling and flexible or accelerated paths, based on my own experience (I left school in 3rd grade, finished high school at 13 and my bachelor's at 17). I'm offering a few free 30-minute chats for parents who'd like to talk through their situation, plus a free checklist. Do you know any parents who might find that helpful? No pressure at all, and I'd love your honest feedback too. [LINK]

## Message B: a parent you know is thinking about it
Hi [Name], I remembered you mentioned [what they said]. I'd be glad to share what worked for me and what I'd do differently, a free 30-minute chat, no pitch. If you'd like, here's a link to pick a time: [LINK]. I also have a free readiness checklist for families: [LINK2].

## Message C: a group admin or community leader (ask first)
Hi [Name], I'm [NAME], a former homeschooler who finished high school at 13 and college at 17. I'd like to share a free checklist for families considering flexible homeschool paths, and answer questions if useful to your members. I want to respect your group's rules. Would that be okay, and where would you want me to share it?

## Message D: after a free chat (a gentle follow-up)
Thanks for talking today, [Name]! Here's the free checklist: [LINK] and the three questions we discussed. If it's helpful, I'd love to hear what was most useful, and what I could do better. No obligation.

## What to ask in every conversation (it improves the guide and the course)
1. What's the hardest part of your situation right now?
2. What have you tried?
3. What would a good outcome look like?
4. What worries you most?
Write their exact words down (with permission). Don't share anyone's story without their written okay.
