What your answers suggest

Not a diagnosis. A starting point for a conversation.

Mostly "Yes"

You have a good base. Next step: write down 3 things you want the learning experience to look like.

A mix of "Not sure"

That's normal. Pick two questions to talk about this week.

Several "No"

That tells you where to start, not whether to stop. Take it one step at a time.

One-page conversation guide

  • Ask the learner: "What is one thing that works for you in how you learn? What is one thing that doesn't?"
  • Write the answers down. Listen first.
  • Change one thing.
  • Check in again in two weeks.

Remember

Homeschooling doesn't have to copy school. It can bend to the child, face to face with an instructor, online with one, or without lectures at all. Ask for feedback, listen, and adjust. Finishing early is one possible outcome, not the goal for every child.
