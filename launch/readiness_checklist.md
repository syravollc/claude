# The 10-Question Readiness Check (free lead magnet, 2 pages)

**For parents and students considering a flexible or accelerated homeschool path.**
By [YOUR NAME]. I left in-person school in 3rd grade, graduated high school at 13 through FLVS Flex, earned my associate degree at 15 and my bachelor's at 17. This is not a test or a diagnosis. It is a set of questions I wish my family had written down, so you can talk it through together.

Answer together. Circle Yes / Not sure / No. There are no scores that tell you what to do.

## About the learner
1. Does the learner say they are bored, stuck, or rushing through things in their current setup? Yes / Not sure / No
2. Can the learner explain what kind of learning works for them (for example: face-to-face instructor, online instructor, reading alone, hands-on)? Yes / Not sure / No
3. Is the learner willing to give honest feedback on what is and is not working, and to try changes? Yes / Not sure / No
4. When something is hard, does the learner keep going, or burn out quickly? (Note what happens, not who is to blame.) Keeps going / Depends / Burns out

## About the family
5. Is at least one adult able to check in on progress regularly, even if they are not teaching? Yes / Not sure / No
6. Are you open to changing the plan when something is not working, instead of repeating how you were taught? Yes / Not sure / No
7. Do you have a plan for social life (clubs, activities, community, friends), not just for academics? Yes / Not sure / No

## About pace and options
8. Do you know the options near you (for example virtual school, online instructors, in-person tutors, dual enrollment)? Yes / Not sure / No
9. Can the learner slow down or take a break without feeling like they failed? Yes / Not sure / No
10. Are you clear that finishing early is one possible outcome, not the goal for every child? Yes / Not sure / No

## What your answers suggest (not a diagnosis)
- Mostly **Yes**: you have a good base. Next step: write down 3 things you want the learning experience to look like.
- Mix of **Not sure**: normal. Pick two questions to talk about this week.
- Several **No**: that tells you where to start, not whether to stop. Take it one step at a time.

## One-page conversation guide
- This week, ask the learner: "What is one thing that works for you in how you learn? What is one thing that does not?"
- Write the answers down. Change one thing. Check in again in two weeks.

## Please note
This checklist is general information from one person's experience. It is not educational, psychological, medical or legal advice. Rules and options vary by state and district. For concerns about learning differences, mental health, or legal requirements, talk to a qualified professional.
