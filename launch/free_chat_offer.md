# The Free Roadmap Chat (free-only mode offer)

Free for now. Paid sessions can come later (see `session_offer.md`). Fill [NAME] and links.

## What it is
A free 20-30 minute conversation with someone who left in-person school in 3rd grade, graduated high school at 13 through FLVS Flex, finished a bachelor's at 17, and is now working on a master's. We talk through your family's situation: learning style, pace, social life and options. No pitch, no pressure.

## What you get
- An honest conversation about what worked for me and what I'd do differently.
- Three questions to take away and talk about as a family.
- The free Readiness Check (if you don't have it yet).
- If useful, a short written summary of the 3-5 things we discussed.

## What it is not
- I'm not a licensed teacher, counselor, psychologist or lawyer. I don't diagnose, give medical or mental-health advice, or tell you what your state requires.
- I can't promise any outcome, including early graduation.
- I'll point you to official sources for rules (your state department of education, your district, the program you're considering).

## Why free?
I'm learning what families most need. In return, I may ask for honest feedback afterward. I will never share your story without your written permission.

## Minors
A parent or guardian books and joins. I keep notes minimal and don't need names, records or identifying details about your child.

## Policies
- Reschedule or cancel any time with 24 hours' notice.
- Calls are not recorded unless you agree in writing.
- I'll never collect payment on this call.
- Privacy: [link]. Notes are deleted after [X] days.

## Short intake form (5 questions, all optional except age/grade)
1. Learner's age or grade (no name needed).
2. Current setup (public school, private, homeschool, virtual, other).
3. What's working now?
4. What isn't?
5. What would you like to talk about?

## Call outline (30 min)
1. (3 min) Welcome, what this is and isn't.
2. (10 min) Their story: what's working, what isn't, what worries them.
3. (10 min) What I'd think about in their place, from my experience. Offer 2-3 ideas, not instructions.
4. (5 min) Three questions to talk about as a family; share the checklist.
5. (2 min) Ask permission for feedback; mention paid sessions only if they ask.

## After the call
- Send the checklist link and your 3 takeaways within 24 hours.
- Log it in your tracker. With permission, note what they said (their words), but don't publish anything identifying.
- Ask: "Was this useful? What was most helpful?" Only quote them with written permission.
