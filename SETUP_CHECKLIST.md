# Setup checklist (free stack), in order

You create the accounts and enter payment/identity details yourself. Nothing here can be done from the cloud session. Prices and limits are from third-party sources: confirm each on the company's own page when you sign up.

## Step 0: decisions before you start (15 min)
- [ ] Business/brand name: ____________  (use it everywhere; no real name needed if you prefer)
- [ ] A free email address for the business (separate from personal).
- [ ] Refund policy decided (suggestion: tell me within 7 days if not useful; offer refund or follow-up).
- [ ] Price: guide $_____ (hypothesis $19-29)  ·  session $_____ (hypothesis $75-150)
- [ ] How you appear on calls: voice only or video.
- [ ] Check local rules: business registration, taxes on digital sales, working with minors' families.

## Step 1: Email list (Kit, free plan)
1. Create the account with your business email.
2. Create a form named "Readiness Check" and an automation: when someone signs up, send welcome email 1.
3. Paste welcome emails from `launch/welcome_emails.md` (3 emails: now, day 3, day 7).
4. Upload `launch/Readiness_Check.pdf` and link it in email 1 (check whether your plan allows file delivery; if not, host the PDF on Payhip as a free product and link it).
5. Fill in [NAME], the disclaimer line, and your reply-to address.
6. Test: sign up with a second email and check all three emails arrive and the link works.
- Verify: current free limit; whether landing pages/forms are on the free plan.

## Step 2: Store (Payhip, free plan)
1. Create the account and the store page.
2. Add "Early, Not Rushed" as a *pre-order / waitlist* until the guide is final (or leave it off until ready).
3. Add the Family Planner (`planner/Family_Planner.pdf`) once you decide whether to sell it or give it as a bonus.
4. Set payout details (Stripe/PayPal), tax settings and your refund policy text.
5. Add your privacy policy and terms links (get these checked if possible).
6. Test a purchase with a discount code at 100% off to confirm delivery.
- Verify: current fee (reported 5% on free plan), whether card fees are extra, what it does for sales tax where you live.

## Step 3: Session booking (Cal.com, free plan)
1. Create the account; create an event "Acceleration Roadmap" (45-60 min).
2. Connect Stripe and require payment at booking.
3. Add the intake questions from `launch/session_offer.md` (8 questions) to the booking form.
4. Add the policies (cancellation 24h, refund, no recording, "I am not a licensed teacher/counselor", parent/guardian on call).
5. Connect your calendar and Zoom/Google Meet.
6. Test: book and pay for a session yourself (then refund it).
- Verify: free plan includes payments and the intake form fields you need.

## Step 4: Landing page
1. Use the copy in `launch/landing_page.md` and the look in `launch/landing_page_preview.html`.
2. Easiest free route: the Payhip store page or Kit's landing page (if on your plan). A separate site builder is optional.
3. Add the signup form (Kit), the booking link (Cal.com) and the waitlist/store link (Payhip).
4. Add the proof image (diploma/transcript/degree with personal details blurred).
5. Footer: privacy, terms, refund, contact, disclaimer.
6. Test on your phone.

## Step 5: Social accounts and posting
1. Create the accounts you will use (suggest: YouTube Shorts, Instagram, TikTok; add Pinterest later). Use the same name and bio everywhere.
2. Bio: one line + link to your landing page.
3. Read each platform's current rules on AI-generated or synthetic media and label accordingly.
4. Set up Metricool free (1 brand, 20 posts a month) and/or schedule natively (YouTube Studio is free).
5. Pin an intro post: use the 60-second version from `STORY_DRAFT.md`.

## Step 6: Content loop (weekly, 30-45 min review + recording)
See `content_pipeline/README.md`.

## Step 7: Soft launch (week 2 onward)
- [ ] Tell people you know about the sessions (message, not mass spam).
- [ ] Post 4x per week.
- [ ] Answer questions in homeschool groups (follow rules; help first, links rarely).
- [ ] Track the gates in `COACHING_BUSINESS_PLAN.md` (day 30 / 60 / 90).

## Cost summary (expected)
$0 per month on free plans. Per sale: Payhip fee (reported 5%) plus card fees; per session: Stripe's card fee. Domain optional. Budget cap stays $300.
