## What the evidence says about an edge

1. **No route showed reliable money for a brand-new seller with no audience and no skill edge.** The best-sounding figures are modeled estimates from companies that sell tools.
2. **The recurring real advantages are not tactics:** (a) free reach, meaning an audience you already have or content that reliably gets views; (b) knowledge of one community so your product, copy and design fit it; (c) a low or zero cost per unit sold, like digital products, so thin margins aren't the problem.
3. **Paid-ad dropshipping has none of those by default.** That is why the earlier math was negative.
4. **Slow organic routes are cheap but small:** Etsy POD and TikTok affiliate cost little money, but the reported first-quarter earnings are roughly $0-300 a month.

## Honest conclusion

On this evidence, nothing reachable from here is likely to make "real money" quickly. The best edge available is one you may already have: an existing audience, a community you belong to, or a skill (for example writing, design, or the homeschool content in your other project). Dropshipping is a way to monetize an edge, not the edge itself.
